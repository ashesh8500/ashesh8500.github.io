AuditLayer

Production software that turns public social signal into sourced competitive audits for wellness creators.

role technical cofounder status in production surface auditlayermedia.com

AuditLayer is the venture I cofounded. Creators in wellness don’t need more dashboards — they need competitive reads they can trust: what rivals publish, what claims show up, and what evidence backs a report before it lands in someone’s inbox.

The production shape

Publicly, the system covers a full loop:

  • Intake — who the audit is for, and what competitive surface matters.
  • Agent runs — orchestrated collection and analysis over public social data.
  • Evidence review — provenance stays attached; founder review can intervene.
  • Billing state — product mechanics, not a research notebook.
  • Private delivery — reports leave through controlled channels, not a public feed.
Peer, don’t reverse-engineer This note describes the product architecture at the level you’d explain to a collaborator. Customer data, prompts, and vendor credentials are not published here.

Why it matters to how I work

Shipping AuditLayer forced the boring virtues: stateful pipelines, failure modes, human-in-the-loop review, and the difference between a demo agent and a system people pay for. That production taste is the counterpart to Fractal’s research honesty and the spatial work’s evaluation discipline.

Where to look next

The live product surface is public. Implementation internals are not. If you want the company-facing story, start at the site; if you want how I think about systems that keep receipts, read Fractal and the systems thesis alongside this.
