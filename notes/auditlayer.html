AuditLayer

Competitive-intelligence software that turns public social data into sourced audits for wellness creators.

Role technical cofounder Status in production Product auditlayermedia.com

AuditLayer is the venture I cofounded. The system covers a full production loop: intake, agent runs over public social data, evidence review with provenance, billing state, and private report delivery through controlled channels.

Production architecture

  • Next.js, Supabase/Postgres, and Python workers.
  • Authenticated intake with queued agent runs.
  • Deterministic orchestration separated from agentic research with bounded execution.
  • Event logs, source provenance, failure states, and founder review controls.

Engineering discipline

Shipping AuditLayer forced stateful pipelines, human-in-the-loop review, and the difference between a demo agent and a system people pay for. That production taste informs Fractal's research honesty and the spatial work's evaluation discipline.

Visibility boundaries

Customer data, internal prompts, and vendor credentials are not published. This note describes the product architecture at the level you would explain to a collaborator.
