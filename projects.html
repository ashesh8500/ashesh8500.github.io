SumCheck accelerator for zero-knowledge proofs

Complete. Team course project, NYU, Spring 2026. Team: Sammy Suliman, Jui-Teng Huang, Ashesh Kaji. Code.

The project implements one programmable SumCheck round over the BN254 scalar field in Vitis HLS. The design follows the datapath of the zkPHIRE paper (HPCA 2026). A Python golden model is the bit-exact reference. It passes randomized round checks for term degrees 2 to 6. The HLS testbench passes 7 of 7 cases on two targets: a PYNQ-Z2 design with one processing element and an RFSoC 4x2 design with eight.

Portfolio allocation as layered control

Complete. Course project, System Optimization Methods, NYU, Spring 2026. Full study. Code.

The study treats portfolio allocation as a layered control problem. It evaluates 60 arms (3 stock universes × 5 screens × 4 controllers) in chronological walk-forward folds. Cost sensitivity is tested up to 25 basis points. A learned RL router improved on a fixed strategy chosen in training. It did not beat a simple trailing-window router.

The study states its limits: survivorship bias in the universes, few independent folds, and a proportional cost model. The study tests the mechanism, not profitability.

AuditLayer

In production. Cofounded in 2026. Role: engineering. auditlayermedia.com.

AuditLayer produces sourced brand and competitor reports from public social media data. Language-model agents do the research inside bounded runs. Deterministic code owns the queue, the storage and the delivery. Each claim in a report keeps a link to its source. The stack is Next.js, Postgres and Python workers.
